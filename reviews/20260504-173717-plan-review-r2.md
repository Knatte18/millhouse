**Location:** Batch 03 / Cards 9 + 10
**Issue:** Card 9 creates `tokens = _build_tokens(resolve_hub_path(), wiki_path, slug=slug)`. Card 10 then creates `dest_tokens = _build_tokens(dest_hub, ...)` and passes `dest_tokens` to `_setup.create_hub_links` *instead of* `tokens`. That leaves the source-side `tokens` with no remaining call sites — `resolve_worktrees_dir` builds its own internal token dict, and portal junction creation uses no token dict. The batch-local decision describes `tokens` as "for source-side junction resolution" but names no concrete call site.
**Fix:** Either remove the `tokens = _build_tokens(resolve_hub_path(), ...)` line in Card 9 (it is never used after Card 10), or add a clarifying note naming the specific use that justifies keeping it.
