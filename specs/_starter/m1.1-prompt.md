**`_subprocess_util.py`** — drop `millpy.core.log_util` imports; replace `log()` calls with `print(f"[subprocess] {msg}", file=sys.stderr)`.

**`_junction.py`** — drop `millpy.core.log_util` and the `_MODULE` constant. Keep the Windows junction support **including** the Python 3.10+ `FILE_ATTRIBUTE_REPARSE_POINT` fallback (already present in legacy — don't lose it during the trim).
