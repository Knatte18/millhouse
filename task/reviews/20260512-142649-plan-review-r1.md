## Findings

### [BLOCKING] Nested-call exception omitted from Batch 1 for mill-plan and mill-start

**Location:** Batch 1 / Card 1

**Issue:** The `nested-call-exception` Shared Decision is declared as "Applies to: mill-go-conversion" only, but mill-plan and mill-start both contain nested `millpy-bg.py` invocations with the same shape. Card 1's four substitution rules — applied mechanically to every matching line — would convert the inner calls (after `-- \`) in mill-plan (Phase: Plan Review steps 2 and 4.5, two occurrences) and mill-start (Phase: Discussion Review step 2, one occurrence) to the PYTHONPATH-prefixed form. millpy-bg.py passes everything after `--` as argv to `subprocess.run`; the first token `PYTHONPATH=...` is treated as the executable name, not an env assignment, and the spawn fails. Both mill-plan and mill-start discussion/plan reviews would be broken post-implementation.

**Fix:** Add a Shape B rule to Card 1 Requirements: "Lines matching the `uv run --project ...` pattern that appear as the command-argument after `-- \` in a `millpy-bg.py` launcher line MUST NOT carry the `PYTHONPATH=` prefix. Convert to `"${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/.venv/Scripts/python.exe" "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/<script>.py" [args]` only (no prefix). The outer `millpy-bg.py` call is Shape A (gets PYTHONPATH prefix); the inner call is Shape B (no prefix)." Update the `nested-call-exception` Shared Decision to read "Applies to: all batches" so the invariant is stated globally.

**Fix:** Update the RIGHT line in bullet 2's bash example to the new direct-venv form, or add a parenthetical clarifying that the example illustrates the cache-path distinction and that the invocation form is governed by bullet 3.
