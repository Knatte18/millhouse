Under that dispatch mode, the first resumed round fails with `ReviewError` before any LLM call. **Fix:** Add a bullet to Card 5's substitution paragraph requiring `--max-rounds <local_max_review_rounds>` on the Subprocess/psmux `millpy-review-plan.py` invocation for the remainder of the resumed loop — mirroring mill-start's own analogous `--max-rounds <max_review_rounds + 1>` extension-round pattern (mill-start/SKILL.md `--auto` non-progress extension), which exists for exactly this backend check.
