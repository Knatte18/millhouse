## Findings

### [NIT] `load_buffer` silently ignores `name` parameter
**Location:** Batch 2, Card 7
**Issue:** `load_buffer(name, buffer_name, file_path)` — the `name` parameter is present in the signature but not used in the constructed argv (`["psmux", "load-buffer", "-b", buffer_name, str(file_path)]`). The test (Card 6) confirms this, but there is no Requirements note explaining the intentional discard.
**Fix:** Add a one-line comment in the Requirements: "psmux `load-buffer` has no `-t` flag; `name` is accepted for API symmetry and is unused."

### [NIT] `_wait_for_idle_prompt` "most recent 10 lines" is underspecified
**Location:** Batch 3, Card 9
**Issue:** The helper spec says "looking for ANY line in the most recent 10 lines" but does not specify that the implementer should slice the capture output as `capture_text.splitlines()[-10:]`. An implementer checking all lines could produce spurious matches if the prompt character appeared in earlier scrollback.
**Fix:** Replace "in the most recent 10 lines" with "in `capture_text.splitlines()[-10:]`" in the helper requirements.

### [NIT] `import os` missing from Card 10 imports list
**Location:** Batch 4, Card 10
**Issue:** Requirements list explicit imports (`shutil`, `subprocess`, `json`, `sys`, `_psmux`) but `os.listdir(HUB)` is used in `test_tool_use` without `import os` listed.
**Fix:** Add `import os` to the imports list.

### [NIT] No test for `send_keys("", enter=False)` → `ValueError`
**Location:** Batch 2, Cards 6–7
**Issue:** Card 7 adds a `ValueError` guard for `keys == "" and enter=False`, but Card 6 has no corresponding test case for this path.
**Fix:** Add one test in Card 6 asserting `send_keys("s1", "", enter=False)` raises `ValueError`.

## Verdict

APPROVE
Plan is sound and well-specified; all findings are NITs with no blocking defects.
