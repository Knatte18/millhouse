## Findings

### [BLOCKING] Blacklist ancestor check has inverted direction
**Location:** Batch 1 / Card 1, Requirements "Blacklist comparison"
**Issue:** `entry in resolved_path.parents` checks whether `entry` is an *ancestor* of `resolved_path`, but the intended guard is the opposite: whether `resolved_path` is an ancestor (parent) of a blacklisted directory. Concretely, for test scenario 5 (`refuses on path ancestor of blacklist`) where `resolved_path = <tmp>` and `entry = <tmp>/container/wiki`, `resolved_path.parents` yields the ancestors of `<tmp>` (e.g. `[Path('/tmp'), Path('/')]`), none of which equal `<tmp>/container/wiki`. No `SystemExit` is raised; the test fails.
**Fix:** Change `entry in resolved_path.parents` to `resolved_path in entry.parents`. The corrected formula `resolved_path == entry or resolved_path in entry.parents` will catch both "delete the wiki" (exact match) and "delete a directory containing the wiki" (resolved_path is an ancestor of a blacklist entry). The same fix must be applied to the identical formula in `discussion.md` → "Path resolution for blacklist check" paragraph.
