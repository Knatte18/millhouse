### [BLOCKING] Card 12's `argument-hint` placement contradicts the convention it cites
**Location:** Batch 5 (`mill-plan-revise-reentry`), Card 12
**Issue:** Card 12 instructs inserting `argument-hint: "[--revise]"` "between `name: mill-plan` and `description: ...`", claiming this matches `mill-setup/SKILL.md`'s convention. But `mill-setup/SKILL.md` (and every other `argument-hint`-bearing `SKILL.md` in the repo — `git-commit`, `git-log`, `git-pr`, `git-clone`, `git-issue`, `mill-start`, `mill-self-report`, `mill-setup`, all at line 4) places `argument-hint:` AFTER `description:`, not between `name:` and `description:`. Following Card 12 literally produces the one frontmatter ordering with zero precedent anywhere in the codebase.
**Fix:** Change the insertion point to immediately after `description: ...` (before the closing `---`), matching the universal `name` → `description` → `argument-hint` order.
