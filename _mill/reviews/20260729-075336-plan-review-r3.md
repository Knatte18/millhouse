## Findings

### [BLOCKING] Partial-restore failure in `check_and_restore` is silently reported as full success
**Location:** batch `treeguard-helper`, Card 1, steps 7-9 (and Card 2's scenario list)
**Issue:** When `deleted_paths` has multiple entries and the single `git checkout HEAD -- <paths>` call restores some but not all of them (partial per-pathspec failure), step 8's "empty `restored_paths` -> diagnostic + `triggered: False`" branch does not fire (restored_paths is non-empty), so step 9 returns `triggered: True` with `restored_paths` silently omitting the still-missing path(s) — no diagnostic is printed anywhere for the leftover deletion. This reproduces, in miniature, the exact "file vanished and nobody noticed" failure this whole task exists to close. Card 2's scenario list (1-9) has no partial-failure test — scenario 7 only covers the all-fail case.
**Fix:** After building `restored_paths`, compute `still_missing = set(deleted_paths) - set(restored_paths)`; if non-empty, print an ASCII diagnostic naming `still_missing` regardless of whether `restored_paths` is also non-empty, and add a unit-test scenario mocking a subprocess success that restores only one of two deleted paths.

### [BLOCKING] Card 8's step 3.6 bracketing references a checkpoint anchor that does not exist
**Location:** batch `mill-go-wiring`, Card 8, "Step 3.6's redispatch also needs its own bracketing" bullet
**Issue:** The card instructs three insertions around step 3.6's "3. Re-run sub-step 3 ... The round counter `H` is not consumed.": a pre-dispatch checkpoint before it, a post-dispatch checkpoint immediately after it and before sub-item 4, AND a third near-duplicate post-dispatch checkpoint "before the `If dispatch == subprocess or psmux:` line that follows it." No such line exists anywhere in step 3.6 (verified against `mill-go/SKILL.md` lines 701-711) — step 3.6 only says "Re-run sub-step 3," it never repeats a literal subprocess/psmux branch of its own (unlike steps 2/3/3.5, where this anchor genuinely exists and the pattern was evidently copy-pasted from). The implementer cannot locate this anchor locally and will either stall or misapply the duplicate sentence at an unrelated `If dispatch == subprocess or psmux:` occurrence elsewhere in the file.
**Fix:** Drop the third insertion instruction entirely — step 3.6's own two checkpoints (pre-dispatch before sub-item 3, post-dispatch after sub-item 3/before sub-item 4) already fully bracket the redispatch; there is no subprocess/psmux branch left to bracket at this cross-reference site.

## Verdict

REQUEST_CHANGES
Two BLOCKING issues: silent partial-restore reporting in `_treeguard.check_and_restore`, and an invalid checkpoint anchor in Card 8's step 3.6.
