### [NIT] Venv-check semantics degrade when `MILL_PYTHON` is unset or stale
**Location:** Batch 3 / Card 5 (mill-go venv-check blocks)
**Issue:** The `replace_all` on `"${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/.venv/Scripts/python.exe"` also rewrites the `if [ ! -f "$MILL_PYTHON" ]` existence checks. If `MILL_PYTHON` is empty (mill-setup not yet run) or points to a deleted old-cache path, the check triggers `uv sync` (which writes to `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/.venv/`), then re-checks `$MILL_PYTHON` (still empty/stale), and halts with "venv not found after sync" — a misleading message. Normal operation (MILL_PYTHON set and valid) is unaffected.
**Fix:** Accept the replacement as-is (functional on the happy path) or scope the two venv-check blocks to keep the explicit path; the latter adds per-card precision but is optional given the rarity of the degraded path.
