### [BLOCKING:design] Cards 3/4 omit the raw-refs-preservation caveat Cards 1/2 include, for an identical hazard
**Location:** Batch 1, Card 3 (`prepare()` holistic) and Card 4 (`run()` holistic)
**Issue:** In `_review_plan.py`, `all_raw_refs` (the single dict Cards 3/4 replace with two split unions) is read again downstream at lines 523/535 (`prepare()`) and 920/932 (`run()`) to build `all_creates_on_disk`/`holistic_moves_on_disk` — the exact same "used again later in the function" hazard Cards 1 and 2 explicitly flag and instruct the implementer to guard against (`raw_refs`/`raw_refs_set` at lines 188/200 and 419/431). Cards 3 and 4's Requirements say only "build two unions... resolve each... concatenate into `all_reads`" with no instruction to preserve a combined lookup for those two later uses.
**Fix:** Add the same caveat Cards 1/2 carry: instruct the implementer to keep a combined `all_context_refs | all_other_refs` (or equivalent) available for the two downstream membership checks currently reading `all_raw_refs`, in both Card 3's and Card 4's Requirements — otherwise a literal implementation raises `NameError` at those lines.

### [NIT:consistency] Cards 1/2's own caveat covers `raw_refs_set` but not the bare `raw_refs` list also read downstream
**Location:** Batch 1, Card 1 (`_review_one_batch()`) and Card 2 (`prepare()` per-batch)
**Issue:** Line 188 (Card 1's site) and line 419 (Card 2's site) read the plain list `raw_refs` (not `raw_refs_set`) via `[raw for raw in combined_creates if raw not in raw_refs]`. Each card's Requirements only says "keep `raw_refs_set` populated as the union..." — the bare-list read is not named, only implicitly covered by the general "check for other uses" instruction.
**Fix:** Explicitly name both `raw_refs` and `raw_refs_set` in the remedy sentence, not just `raw_refs_set`, so the implementer doesn't have to infer that a set can stand in for the list read.

### [NIT:consistency] Card 9's "bootstrap card for both Card 9 and Card 10" framing overstates Card 9's own exposure to the validator check
