## Findings

### [NIT] Card 3 — resolve_deps_as_names already imported
**Location:** Batch 2, Card 3
**Issue:** The plan instructs to "Update the imports near line 35 to also import `resolve_deps_as_names` from `_plan_dag`," but `resolve_deps_as_names` is already present in the `_plan_validate.py` import line: `from _plan_dag import PlanDAGError, extract_batch_index, resolve_deps_as_names`. The instruction is a no-op and will confuse the implementer.
**Fix:** Remove the instruction to add the import; note in requirements that the import already exists and only the new function body and `run()` registration are needed.

### [NIT] Card 5 — _collect_task_intent inline-form path extraction underspecified
**Location:** Batch 3, Card 5
**Issue:** The requirements say to extract lines matching `^-\s*\*\*(Edits|Creates|Deletes):\*\*` "and the indented sub-bullet block that follows," which covers only multi-line form. Plan files commonly use the inline form (`- **Deletes:** \`path/file.py\``). An implementer focused on sub-bullets will miss inline paths, causing the DU-conflict resolver to miss intentional deletions.
**Fix:** Explicitly state that the `(?P<inline>.*)` capture group of `_RE_REFS_HEADER` must also be checked — if non-empty, extract backtick tokens from it directly before scanning sub-bullets (mirrors the existing `_parse_edits_only` pattern in `_plan_validate.py`).

### [NIT] Card 9 — resolve_git_root/resolve_wiki_path signatures placed after step 3 but used in step 1
**Location:** Batch 5, Card 9 (both SKILL.md files)
**Issue:** The plan places the new `_paths.resolve_git_root` and `_paths.resolve_wiki_path` signature lines immediately after the `_config.load_config` line in Entry step 3, but both functions are first called in Entry step 1 (`_paths.resolve_wiki_path(_paths.resolve_git_root())`). The misalignment makes the inline-signature convention harder to read.
**Fix:** Place the two new signature lines immediately below the `_wiki.sync_pull` signature in Entry step 1, where the functions are actually invoked.
