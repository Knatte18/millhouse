### [NIT] mill-groom Step 3 scope table retains `task.phase` after dict conversion
**Location:** Batch 2, Card 9
**Issue:** Card 9 replaces Step 2 with `_client.list_tasks_brief` (returns dicts with `"status"` key), but Step 3's scope rules table header still reads `| \`task.phase\` | Listed? |`. **Fix:** In Card 9's Requirements, add: update the Step 3 scope table column header from `` `task.phase` `` to `` `task["status"]` ``.

### [NIT] mill-setup Phase 8 verification bullets not updated by Card 13
**Location:** Batch 4, Card 13
**Issue:** Card 13 removes `Home.md starts with # Tasks` and `_Sidebar.md begins with ### Navigation` from Phase 5's checklist, but the identical two bullets remain in Phase 8's comprehensive Verify+report checklist. **Fix:** In Card 13's Requirements, add: also remove (or replace with a daemon-based equivalent such as `` `_client.list_tasks_brief(wiki_path)` returns without error``) the same two bullets from Phase 8's verification list.
