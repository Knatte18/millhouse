## Findings

### [NIT:consistency] `<branch>` positional-arg doc goes stale after Card 1
**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** Batch 1, Card 1 + Card 2 (mill-merge/SKILL.md Step 2, mill-merge-in/SKILL.md Entry step 3).
**Issue:** Entry step 3's current text describes `<branch>` as "for ad-hoc syncing from some other branch than the task's declared parent." After Card 1, mill-merge's *normal, every-call* path now passes the task's own resolved declared parent through this exact argument — directly contradicting "some other branch than the task's declared parent." Neither card instructs updating this sentence.
**Fix:** Add a requirement to Card 2 (or Card 1) to revise the `<branch>` description to cover both the pre-existing ad-hoc-override use and the new caller-handoff use.

### [NIT:consistency] Card 2's "mirrors mill-merge Step 4" claim overstates the analogy
**Location:** Batch 1, Card 2 (mill-merge-in/SKILL.md Entry).
**Issue:** Card 2 says it "mirrors" `mill-merge/SKILL.md` Entry Step 4's status_path-absent exemption, but Step 4 skips its liveness check *entirely* for that branch ("This liveness check applies only to the `status_path.exists()` True branch"), whereas Card 2's design keeps the liveness check running in both branches and exempts only the rebind write. The actual instructions in Card 2 are unambiguous, but the cited precedent doesn't match, which could mislead an implementer double-checking against Step 4.
**Fix:** Reword the "mirror" sentence to state plainly that this is a deliberately different (broader) exemption scope than Step 4's, not a mirror of it.

### [NIT:consistency] Reorder in Card 2 doesn't address Step 4's step-2 cross-reference
**Location:** Batch 1, Card 2 vs. mill-merge-in/SKILL.md Step 4 (Verify), which reads "`_paths.resolve_task_path(hub_root, "_mill/status.md")` (the same resolution Entry step 2 already uses)."
**Issue:** Card 2 reorders/merges Entry steps 2 and 3 but gives the implementer no explicit instruction to verify or renumber this downstream "Entry step 2" citation in Step 4, which is outside Card 2's described edit but lives in the same file.
**Fix:** Add one sentence to Card 2 requiring the implementer to confirm/update the Step 4 cross-reference after renumbering.

### [NIT:design] Card 5's citation-scan warning text is imprecise for the restore-from-base branch
**Location:** Batch 2, Card 5 (mill-finalize/SKILL.md Step 3).
**Issue:** The proposed warning says the link "is about to go dead," but on the `base_tracks_task_dir` True (restore) branch, `<task_dir>` isn't removed — it's repopulated with `<parent_branch>`'s own tree at that path. A citation to `_mill/discussion.md` there doesn't 404; it silently starts pointing at a *different* task's discussion content, not a dead link.
**Fix:** Split the warning wording: "removed" branch → "about to go dead"; "restore" branch → "about to point at a different task's content."

### [NIT:consistency] `_explicit_scope`'s `toplevel` param becomes dead after the fix
**Location:** Batch 3, Card 7 (resolve_scope.py `_explicit_scope`).
**Issue:** Card 7 has the function accept a new `cwd_path` and anchor tokens to it instead of `toplevel`, but doesn't say whether to keep or drop the now-unused `toplevel` parameter.
**Fix:** State explicitly whether `toplevel` should be dropped from `_explicit_scope`'s signature or kept for call-site symmetry.
