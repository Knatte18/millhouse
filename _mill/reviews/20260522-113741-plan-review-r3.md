## Findings

### [BLOCKING] `test-wiki-sync.py` auto-included by `run-all.py` glob
**Location:** Batch 6, Card 9 + Card 11
**Issue:** Card 9 places `test-wiki-sync.py` in `plugins/mill/unit_tests/`; `run-all.py` discovers tests via `HERE.glob("test-*.py")` — no exclusion mechanism exists. Card 11 says "Do NOT add `test-wiki-sync.py`" but the glob will include it automatically, violating the no-real-git constraint for `run-all.py`.
**Fix:** Card 11 Requirements must explicitly add a `SKIP = {"test-wiki-sync.py"}` set (or equivalent exclusion) to `run-all.py` and filter it from the glob results before running.

### [BLOCKING] `Store.content_hash` must be `@staticmethod`; plan specifies instance method
**Location:** Batch 1 Card 2 (definition), Batch 4 Card 5 (usage), Batch 6 Card 7 (test)
**Issue:** Card 2 defines `content_hash(self, content: str) -> str` as a plain instance method. Card 5's `_handle_write` CAS check calls it as `Store.content_hash(disk_content)` (no instance), and Card 7 test case 6 calls it the same way — both fail at runtime with `TypeError` for a non-static method.
**Fix:** Card 2 Requirements must add `@staticmethod` to `content_hash` (signature becomes `content_hash(content: str) -> str`); Card 5 and Card 7 usages are then correct as written.

## Verdict

REQUEST_CHANGES
Two blocking issues: `run-all.py` glob exclusion gap and `Store.content_hash` staticmethod omission.
