## Findings

### [BLOCKING] Batch 4 DAG missing `depends-on: [3]`
**Location:** `00-overview.md` batch index; Card 10 Context
**Issue:** `00-overview.md` declares batch 4 `depends-on: []`, so mill-go may schedule it before batch 3 completes; Card 10's `Context:` includes `plugins/mill/scripts/millpy-claude-sub.py`, which batch 3 Card 8 creates.
**Fix:** Change batch 4's `depends-on:` to `[3]` in `00-overview.md`.

### [BLOCKING] `duration` NameError in response-poll timeout path
**Location:** Batch 3, Card 9, step 11
**Issue:** `duration` is assigned only in the success branch (`On success: compute duration = ...`); the `MarkerNotFoundError` timeout-raise references `{duration:.1f}s`, producing a Python `NameError` on the first timeout.
**Fix:** Replace `{duration:.1f}s` in the `RuntimeError` message with `{time.monotonic() - start:.1f}s`, or bind `elapsed = time.monotonic() - start` before the try/except.

### [BLOCKING] `set_history_limit` call absent from wrapper flow
**Location:** Batch 3, Card 9
**Issue:** The discussion's technical context explicitly requires `set-option history-limit 50000` once at startup (with fallback policy); Card 9's 12-step flow never calls `_psmux.set_history_limit`. Without it, long responses may be silently truncated at the pane's default scrollback.
**Fix:** Add `_psmux.set_history_limit(session_name, 50000)` immediately after `new_session` in Card 9 step 6.

### [NIT] Batch 3 header `depends-on` inconsistent with overview DAG
**Location:** `03-wrapper.md` YAML header
**Issue:** `03-wrapper.md` declares `depends-on: []`; the authoritative overview DAG declares `depends-on: [1, 2]`.
**Fix:** Change `03-wrapper.md` header to `depends-on: [1, 2]`.

### [NIT] Card 5 stub omits `import _subprocess_util`
**Location:** Batch 2, Card 5
**Issue:** Card 6 patches `_psmux._subprocess_util.run`; if `_psmux` never imports `_subprocess_util` in the stub, the mock raises `AttributeError` at patch time rather than the expected `NotImplementedError`.
**Fix:** Add `import _subprocess_util` and `import sys` to Card 5's module-level import requirements so Card 6's mocks resolve correctly against the stub.

## Verdict

REQUEST_CHANGES — three blocking issues; two are single-line additions.
